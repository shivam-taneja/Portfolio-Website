    # Moo-ve It

    - [View live project](https://mooveit.shivamtaneja.com/)

    <SubSection title="About The Project">
      Moo-ve It is an absurd endless runner. You play as a balloon cow running on four pink teats, jumping wooden fences before gravity turns you into a pancake.

      The original inspiration was [this post on X](https://x.com/AIMind_Ai/status/2107435499686154557) — a balloon cow on teat-legs that was too ridiculous not to turn into a game.

      The game runs entirely in the browser on a canvas. Score climbs as the pasture scrolls faster, slow-moo kicks in at milestones, and a high score sticks in local storage. Day and night themes swap the sky while the cow keeps looking at your cursor.

      Desktop only — it needs at least a 960px-wide window. Phones get a polite “bigger pasture” message instead.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="Features">
      - **Endless runner**: Jump fences with Space, ↑, or tap. Speed ramps up as you go.
      - **Slow moo**: Milestone hits freeze the moment with a slow-moo flash.
      - **Death theater**: Random pancake / curdle / dairy-disaster lines when you splat.
      - **Look-at-you cow**: Eyes track the cursor; death gets a cute “looks at you” expression.
      - **Day / night pasture**: Theme toggle between sunny field and midnight moo.
      - **Local high score**: Best run saved in the browser.
      - **Desktop gate**: Blocks mobile and undersized windows with a clear minimum width.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="Technologies Used">
      - [TanStack Start](https://tanstack.com/start) + React + TypeScript
      - [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com)
      - HTML5 Canvas for gameplay
      - Hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com)
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="How to Play">
      - Open it on a PC, laptop, or wide tablet (960px+).
      - Press Space or ↑ (or tap the canvas) to start and jump.
      - Clear the fences. Don't pancake.
    </SubSection>

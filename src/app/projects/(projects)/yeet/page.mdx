    <SubSection title="About The Project">
      Yeet auto cross-posts between X and Threads. Write on X, it posts to Threads by itself. Write on Threads, it lands on X. Text and images included. Free and open source.

      It runs in the browser as an extension for Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. When you post on X or Threads, Yeet captures the text (and images, if Copy Images is on), opens a background tab on the other platform, fills the composer, and posts using the session you are already logged into. There is no Yeet server and no database — nothing about the post is uploaded to us.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="How It Works">
      - Post on X, and it lands on Threads. Post on Threads, and it lands on X.
      - Enable Copy Images in Settings and photos hop over too (up to four on X). Videos and GIFs are not copied yet.
      - Yeet uses the accounts you are already logged into. It never asks for passwords or stores login tokens.
      - You can pause Yeet and check the last yeet without leaving the page you are on.
    </SubSection>

    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="Features">
      - **Two-way by default**: X to Threads and Threads to X
      - **Text and images**: Copy Images in Settings carries photos both ways
      - **Posts by itself**: Background tab fills the other composer and posts
      - **Write once**: The same words land on both timelines
      - **No copy-paste**: No re-typing and no second post to remember
      - **Runs locally**: No server, no stored posts on our side
      - **Chrome, Edge, and Firefox**
      - **Free and open source**

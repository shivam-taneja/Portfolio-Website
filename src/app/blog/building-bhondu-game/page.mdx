    # Building Bhondu Game

    <div className="text-sm dark:text-neutral-500 text-neutral-500">
      November 23, 2026
    </div>

    Bhondu Life is a desi turn-based life simulator I built. On this site the
    project is Bhondu Game. Each day gives you a new situation. You make a
    choice, and that choice shapes how the story ends. The days come from
    college lectures, office drama, shaadi pressure, and uncle encounters.

    - [Play Bhondu Life](https://www.bhondugame.com/)
    - [Project page](https://www.shivamtaneja.com/projects/bhondu-game)

    <SubSection title="Why I built Bhondu Game">
      I built Bhondu Life around one repeated day. A situation shows up, taken
      from desi life. You pick a funny choice, a jugaad choice, or a cringey
      choice, and you earn Bhondu Points. College, office, shaadi, and uncle
      mode are the stages you move through. Daily kalesh is part of that day.
      Achievements unlock as you play, and a leaderboard ranks players toward
      Param Bhondu.

      If you searched for bhondu game, bhondu life, or bhondugame and landed
      here, this is that project.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="What you can do">
      - Play a fresh situation each day, based on desi life
      - Move through college, office, shaadi, and uncle mode
      - Make a funny, jugaad, or cringey choice and earn Bhondu Points
      - Track stats, including Jugaad, Kalesh, and Chill
      - Handle daily kalesh
      - Unlock mini-games and achievements as you play
      - Climb a leaderboard with other players
      - Play as a guest, or sign in to save progress

      You can play the web app, or download the mobile apps. Guest play works
      without an account. Sign in when you want that progress saved.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="How Bhondu Game is built">
      The project page splits the build into three parts. Mobile apps use React
      Native. The web app uses Next.js. The backend and the database use NestJS
      and Firebase.

      Stack:
      - React Native
      - Next.js
      - NestJS
      - Firebase
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="What I learned">
      Guest play has to stand on its own. You can start without an account.
      Sign-in is there so progress can be saved.

      The build has three parts. React Native for the mobile apps, Next.js for
      the web app, and NestJS with Firebase for the backend and the data. The
      loop a player sees is a situation, a choice, and Bhondu Points. The rank
      you play toward is Param Bhondu.
    </SubSection>

    <SubSection title="Try Bhondu Game">
